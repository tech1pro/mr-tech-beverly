Common questions

Before you bring your computer in.

Do I need an appointment?

Text Ricky first to explain the issue and confirm a time to bring your computer in.

What should I include in my text?

Send a short description, a photo of the problem if it is visible, and a clear close-up of the model label. On laptops, the label is usually on the bottom. Make sure the brand and model number are readable; if the label is missing, send what you have.

How do replacement parts work?

Ricky helps identify the correct part and sends you a purchase link when a replacement is needed. You buy the part directly, then arrange installation with the shop. Check with Ricky before ordering so the part matches your computer.

Can you help with a slow computer?

Yes. A slow computer can be caused by software, storage, memory, heat, or aging hardware. Mr. Tech can identify the likely cause and explain practical options.

Do you work on laptops and desktop PCs?

Yes. Mr. Tech helps with both laptops and desktop computers, including Windows troubleshooting, upgrades, cleanup, and repair.

Can you recover my files?

Mr. Tech can assess the drive and discuss recovery options. Data recovery depends on the condition of the device, so it cannot be promised in advance.

Do you build custom gaming PCs?

Yes. Mr. Tech can help plan, build, upgrade, and tune a custom gaming PC in Beverly based on your goals and budget.

Do you support small businesses?

Yes. Small business tech support is available for workstations, basic networks, maintenance, backups, and everyday computer problems.
